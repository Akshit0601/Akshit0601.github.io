SHEET — 01 / ABOUT

About

My graduate work is in Intelligent Systems, Robotics & Control, and it's where most of my curiosity lives. I'm drawn to perception and planning, and to model-free reinforcement learning in particular, where an agent figures things out through experience instead of being handed a neat equation. I love algorithms that mirror how people think and act: noticing, deciding, adjusting, trying again. Simulation is where I like to watch those ideas come alive before they meet the real world.

That curiosity has taken me through computer vision, robotics hardware like Raspberry Pi and Jetson, and simulation engines including Gazebo, MuJoCo, and PyBullet, all tied together with ROS and ROS 2. I've built and learned along the way through a full-time role, internships, and hands-on projects, and I enjoy the stretch between an idea working in simulation and working on real hardware.
